## Overview

Micro-frontends partition ownership/deployment across teams via module federation/iframes/routes stitching. Real costs: duplicated dependencies, cross-cutting consistency challenges, complex orchestration. Adopt for organizational scale problems, not technical elegance — most apps benefit from modular monolith discipline instead.
