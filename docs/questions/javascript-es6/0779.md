# What does lexical vs dynamic scoping look like in practice, and when does it matter?

> **Category:** JavaScript & ES6 | **Difficulty:** Medium | **Question #779**

## Overview

JavaScript resolves identifiers lexically: a function’s scope chain is fixed by where it was written, not where it runs. That is why callbacks retain access to defining-scope variables even when invoked elsewhere, and why passing functions around preserves their closure environment predictably.
